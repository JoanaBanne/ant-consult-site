Our expertise

Six services, one engineering standard

Each service covers a distinct stage of an irrigation project, from testing whether a development is worth the capital through to keeping a built scheme performing. They work on their own, or in sequence.

01
Feasibility studies

Evaluate project viability before committing capital. We assess development options against real financial metrics so you can invest with confidence, or redirect before it costs you.

Learn more →
02
Masterplans

A phased development plan built around your operation. We turn feasibility studies into practical implementation plans that align technical priorities with financial reality.

Learn more →
03
Commercial irrigation design

The right system for your crop, your land, your operation. We design drip, micro, centre pivot and sprinkler systems that are practical, reliable and engineered to minimise lifetime operating costs.

Learn more →
04
Irrigation upgrades

Improve performance without replacing what still works. We assess low pressure, uneven application and ageing infrastructure to identify targeted upgrades that deliver the greatest return on investment.

Learn more →
05
Bulk water supply

Move water efficiently from source to scheme. We design bulk pipelines and pump stations engineered for reliable operation, low lifecycle cost and straightforward maintenance.

Learn more →
06
System assessments

Identify problems before they become expensive failures. We assess hydraulic performance, operational constraints and system reliability to recommend practical improvements that extend the life of your infrastructure.

Designs that hold up on site

Every service ends in something a contractor can build from: construction-ready drawings, a bill of quantities that prices accurately, and a design report that explains why the system is what it is.

Service levels

How far you want us to carry the project

Every level includes the full design process and its deliverables. What changes is how much of the construction phase we stay involved in.

Essential
Design only

Our baseline package, for the operation that says "point me in the right direction and I'll handle it". Everything you need to construct a hydraulically excellent irrigation system on your own.

Includes
Application rate determination
Lifecycle cost optimised infield design, from farthest emitter to pumpstation exit flange
Lifecycle cost optimised mainline design
Pumpstation design: filtration, fertigation, pump selection, suction and structural design
Outputs: report, drawings, bills of quantities and specifications
2 site visits and weekly clarification meetings
Explore plan
Professional
Design + build support

For self-sufficient operations installing complex systems. Assurance that construction stays on track while you handle day-to-day management and quality control.

Everything in Essential, plus
Senior engineer site visits during construction and commissioning (3 of): at kickoff, midway and for commissioning
Priority response support: one-day turnaround on all queries during the construction period
Explore plan
Enterprise
Design + full delivery

A full Engineering, Procurement and Construction Management package for large corporate agricultural operations looking for plug and play systems built to high standards. Only suitable for big projects.

Everything in Professional, plus
Facilitation of the procurement process
Full time construction supervision, from construction kickoff through to commissioning
