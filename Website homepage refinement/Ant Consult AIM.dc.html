The ANT Implementation Method (AIM)

A structured approach to getting projects right the first time

Every project has more than one right answer. AIM finds yours.

Agricultural water projects involve multiple stakeholders, competing priorities and a scope that keeps moving as the design develops. AIM brings the right people around the table at the right time, clarifies what the operation actually needs before the design is finalised, and delivers an outcome the full management team can stand behind.

How AIM works differently

Unlike traditional approaches that attempt to define every requirement before design begins, AIM uses an initial design as a practical tool for testing assumptions. We use that draft as the focus of a structured review session where the full client team, Projects, Operations, and management, pulls it apart together.

Identifying what does not work early is often the fastest way to define the right solution. That session gives us a clear, agreed brief that the final design is built from, eliminating the iteration cycles that come from trying to reverse-engineer requirements after the fact.
