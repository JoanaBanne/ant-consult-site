Tools

Three calculators, free to use

Most irrigation decisions come down to a number someone has not worked out yet: what the design will cost, what the pipe will cost to run, what the whole thing returns.

These are the tools we use in our own work. No account, no sales call attached.

Services Cost Estimator

Establish the ballpark design cost of a new irrigation development or an upgrade. Available in ZAR for Southern Africa and in USD elsewhere.

Open the estimator ↗
Pipe Lifecycle Cost Analysis

Compare pipe diameters and classes on what they cost to own, not what they cost to buy. The cheapest pipe on the quote is rarely the cheapest pipe to pump through.

Irrigation ROI Calculator

Model capital cost, operating cost and return side by side, so you can see which system option actually pays and over what period.
